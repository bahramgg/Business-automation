## The problem

Orders came from three places: phone, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp. During the
rush nobody had time to reply, and messages sent after closing were effectively
lost. The menu lived in three places and the prices disagreed.

## What was built

A lightweight site with an always-current menu, and an answering assistant on
Telegram and a site widget. The assistant reads the menu and prices from that
single source, handles the recurring questions — hours, delivery area, table
reservations — and logs the order with its details. Orders land in a customer
database at the same time.

## Result

Busy hours no longer drop messages, and late-night questions aren't unanswered
by morning. The menu has one source of truth; a price change happens once.
